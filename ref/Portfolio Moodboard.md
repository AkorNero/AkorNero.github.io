## Where to start

Your story is "a mechanical engineer who makes AI behave like engineering: measurable, safe, built to ship." These three mockups lean into that.

1. **Lab Notebook (strongest fit).** Family A on its own, with the Dymas-style giant name in the hero. Experience becomes annotated "Fig." plates, Impact numbers become measurement readouts, the KAN thesis gets a diagram plate.
2. **Signal from noise (dark alternative).** Family B for the hero and Research sections (your photo resolving from halftone dots on load), Family A's grid and mono labels for everything else. Reads as AI without the purple-glow cliché.
3. **Night to dawn (the calm one).** Family C, where the About chapters (2018 to now) move the sky from night to sunrise as you scroll. Pair with the light layout references for clean cards.

**Notes**

- `canvas.png` is a blank white image, so it is not in any family.
- Family F is the most common look for AI portfolios; use it as an accent palette inside another family rather than a full look.
